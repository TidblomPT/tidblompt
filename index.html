About

Victor J Tidblom AB is a Swedish limited company developing digital products and services within fitness, health, and personal coaching. We build tools that help individuals improve their routines, training, and overall wellbeing through structured digital support.

Our work focuses on digital coaching experiences, training programs, and health-related software products designed for private individuals. We aim to create practical, user-friendly tools that support healthier habits and long-term progress.

Services

  • Digital coaching programs for fitness and nutrition
  • Online personal coaching through app and web
  • Development of health-related digital products and services
